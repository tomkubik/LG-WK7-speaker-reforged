Speaker

This slider sets the WK7's level for moOde (HTTP stream and Multiroom), default 40. It is independent of moOde's own volume knob. It never affects AirPlay or Spotify: while one of them plays, the phone's, Mac's or Spotify app's volume controls the speaker and the speaker's buttons nudge it. When it stops or pauses, moOde comes back at this level.
Changing name, AirPlay version or maximum volume restarts AirPlay (a few seconds); changing name or Spotify Connect restarts Spotify.

moOde

Advanced: moOde buffering (tap to show)
Smaller buffers make moOde's pause/skip react faster on the WK7, but may cause dropouts on busy Wi-Fi. In HTTP-stream mode, most of the delay comes from moOde's stream itself; only the playback buffer applies here. Total delay added by the WK7 is roughly network + playback buffer.
Applying restarts the moOde receiver; playback resumes within a second or two.
